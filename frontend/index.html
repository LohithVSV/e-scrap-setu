How it works

Four steps, one register — from a citizen's phone to the Commissioner's screen.

01

Find & scan

Citizen finds the nearest drop-off on the map and scans its QR code.

02

Deposit & verify

Field officer confirms the deposit; it's logged to the digital register instantly.

03

Earn credits

City sees it live

  • No citizen rewards tied to municipal collection points
  • No QR-based navigation to the nearest drop-off
